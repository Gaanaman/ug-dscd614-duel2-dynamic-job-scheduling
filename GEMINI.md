# Academic Writing Guidelines

When generating, editing, or suggesting text for this project, you MUST strictly adhere to the following human-authored academic writing conventions. Your output must read like it was written by a human computer science researcher, entirely devoid of "AI-isms" or "LLM slop."

## 1. Tone and Structure
- **Terse and Direct**: Be information-dense. State the analytical position directly without hedging or circling the topic.
- **Vary Sentence Length**: Avoid robotic rhythms (e.g., identical-length sentences). Mix short, punchy statements with longer structural ones.
- **No "Hedge-then-Assert"**: Stop writing structures like "While X is true, Y is better." 
- **No Triads**: Stop using three-item descriptive lists (e.g., "fast, reliable, and scalable"). Pick the most accurate descriptor and drop the rest.
- **Active Voice**: Favor active voice over passive voice when possible, keeping sentences clear and forceful.

## 2. Banned Vocabulary (The "No-Slop" List)
Do not use any of the following statistically overused AI trigger words or filler phrases:
- **Verbs**: delve, leverage, utilize (use "use" instead), facilitate, elucidate, streamline, showcase, harness.
- **Adjectives/Nouns**: robust, crucial, pivotal, seamless, comprehensive, myriad, landscape, "intricacies of", cutting-edge, transformative, innovative.
- **Filler Transitions**: furthermore, moreover, additionally, "it is worth noting that," "it is important to note," "as previously mentioned," "in today's rapidly evolving."
- **Dead-Weight Phrases**: "it is well known that," "studies have shown," "this study aims to."

## 3. Project-Specific Prose Conventions (`.prose-conventions`)
- **No Meta-Commentary**: The report states findings, not how the document was assembled. Do not narrate the document (e.g., ban "This report shows," "This section will explore").
- **No Bold Text**: Strictly forbid bold text in body prose, including run-in headings.
- **Absolute Metrics**: Use absolute values or decimal differences. Never use 'points' or 'pts' as a metric unit.
- **No Commentary on Writing Process**: Do not mention things like "verified against Crossref" or "this report was verified" in the body prose.

## Execution
Review your output before responding. If your text sounds chatty, uses "furthermore", "delve", or explains what a section will do, rewrite it to be factual, direct, and terse.
